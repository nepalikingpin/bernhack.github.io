What is BernHack?

Nepal's most innovative hackathon bringing together the brightest minds to solve real-world problems

BernHack 2025 is Nepal's premier hackathon that brings together 500+ developers, designers, and innovators for 48 hours of intense coding, creativity, and collaboration. As MLH's official member event in Nepal, we're committed to fostering innovation and building the next generation of tech leaders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BernHack 2025

A hackathon is a 48-hour event where developers, designers, and innovators come together to build creative solutions to real-world problems. It's about rapid prototyping, learning, and collaboration.

BernHack is open to all students, professionals, and tech enthusiasts. Whether you're a beginner or expert, there's a place for you. Teams can have 2-4 members.

No! BernHack is completely free for all participants. We provide meals, accommodation, swag, and all the resources you need during the event.

Bring your laptop, chargers, any hardware you might need, and your enthusiasm! We'll provide everything else including food, drinks, and workspace.

You can register individually or with a team. If you don't have a team, we'll help you find teammates during the team formation session at the beginning of the event.

We provide accommodation at the college for participants who need it. The venue is open 24/7 during the hackathon, so you can work, rest, and collaborate anytime.
